// LABS EXPERIMENT 01 — the lattice

FRIENDS WE BUILD FOR, AND HOW THEIR WORK RHYMES.

This is drasticstatic labs' own map, not anyone else's. Every node is a project we have been lucky to help. The lines are rhymes we noticed: an art studio and a vinyl shop both put color on surfaces; a retreat and a wrap shop both own vehicles. Nothing here is a commitment, a partnership, or a request. Mycelium connects by growing toward what feeds it, and so do we.

Taylor'd Wraps appears here as a new friend, with no obligations. Chris has not even met Taylor yet. If you are Taylor: hello, this page is the labs thinking out loud, and you can ask us to take you off it any time.
