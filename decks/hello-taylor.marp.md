# Who we are, in three lines

- **Chris W** looks after websites and helpers for a few organisations around Kenney (the foundation, the retreats, a fellowship). The lab is called **drasticstatic**.
- We build **showcases**: real, working sites made as gifts, so a shop can see what's possible before deciding anything.
- **You approve everything.** If it ever goes further, every word and photo on it is yours to bless.

---

# What was built, in two days

| Site | What it does | Open it |
|---|---|---|
| 🚗 | **THE CARPORT**, the real-site candidate: a 3D van that wraps itself in colour-flip film while you orbit it; a booth that lays film on fifteen things, or your own photo; a counter that ends in a numbered ticket and a phone call | https://drasticstatic.github.io/taylordwraps-nextjs-preview/ |
| 🎪 | **THE CAR SHOW**, the hand-built first version kept alive as an arcade: the original peel, a vinyl toolbox, three games, every draft, the lab's experiments | https://drasticstatic.github.io/taylordwraps-public-preview/ |

Both work on a phone. Both are demo photos until you share yours.

---

# What it does not do, on purpose

- **No payments, anywhere.** The sites are hosted on GitHub Pages, which forbids stores. Commerce stays on **taylordwraps.com**.
- **Everything is a reason to call.** Every path ends at your number, not a checkout.
- **Nothing is collected.** No accounts, no tracking, no forms that go anywhere.
- **It can be turned off with one message.**

---

# What's possible, if you ever want to work together

- **Your real photos** in the booth and the peel: the whole thing gets ten times better.
- **Your words** on every page, in your voice; a *Bienvenidos* page is already there for customers who prefer Spanish.
- **Your own address** on the front of it (taylordwraps.com, or a new one), when you want.
- **A real storefront, off GitHub**, only if you ever want one; we wrote down where it could live and what it would cost, with card data never touching our code.
- **Nothing locks you in.** If you ever leave your current store, there's a one-page note for that day too.

# If you ever want to give something back

- **You don't have to.** The gift is complete as it is.
- **For the work itself** (changes, a real site, the storefront one day), you'd pay Chris directly: the **donate button** is on his page, [drasticstatic.github.io/resume](https://drasticstatic.github.io/resume/).
- **Chris gives a share of what he earns in service to The Holy Earth Foundation**, Kenney's foundation. That is his own gift, made after the fact; a payment to him is not itself a donation to the foundation.
- **And for your own shop:** the same button can go on your site, so a happy customer can bless you beyond the invoice. It's already built; we'd just turn it on when you say so.

---

# Keeping it alive, if it ever goes real

A gift is a moment; a website is a habit. If the showcase ever becomes your real site, here is what keeps it honest and sustainable:

| Maintenance option | Included |
|---|---|
| **Hosting** | stays free on GitHub Pages; your own address costs about **$12 a year** |
| **Small changes** (a photo, a price, a line of text) | you send them through Kenney; we make them; no charge |
| **Bigger work** (a new section, a booking flow, a storefront off GitHub) | we write down what it is and what it would cost **before** anything is built; you say yes or no |
| **If we ever can't** | every file is yours, in plain text, with its history; any web person can pick it up in an afternoon |
| **Our ask for the long run** | keep the photos coming, and tell us when something feels off; that's the whole maintenance plan |
