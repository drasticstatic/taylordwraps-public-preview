// LABS EXPERIMENT 03 — Wrap Origin v2

PROVENANCE YOU CAN CHECK YOURSELF.

Three steps, all in your browser. Hash a file. Compare it to the manifest this site shipped with. Sign a statement with your own wallet that says "this hash, this design, this moment." No token is minted, nothing is written to a chain, nothing costs anything. The statement is a signature you can hand to anyone, and anyone with the standard tools can check who signed it.

This is an experiment from drasticstatic labs. It proves a design existed and who vouched for it. It does not transfer ownership of anything, and it is not an NFT.
